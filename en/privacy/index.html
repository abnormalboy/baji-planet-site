On-device data

The app processes photos you import, collection fields, framing, materials, links, and local change times, and stores them in the system-isolated app sandbox. Your original album or files are not overwritten.

Network and third parties

The current 1.0.0 version has no accounts, cloud sync, ads, analytics, push, crash reporting, or payments. 3D loads only bundled models and on-device textures.

Permissions

Photos and camera are requested only when you choose those entry points. The file picker only accesses the image or collection pack you select.

Export, deletion, and retention

A collection pack includes original images. Clearing the full collection requires two confirmations. The app has no server copy.

Changes

If cloud sync, analytics, ads, or AI services are added, this policy will be updated before that version ships.
