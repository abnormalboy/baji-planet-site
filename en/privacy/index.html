On-device data

The app processes photos you import, collection fields, composition, materials, links, and local change times, and stores them in the system-isolated app sandbox. Original albums and files are not overwritten.

Network and third parties

The current 1.0.0 version has no accounts, cloud sync, ads, analytics, push, crash reporting, or payments. 3D loads only in-package models and on-device textures.

Permissions

Photos and camera are requested only when you choose those entry points; the file picker only accesses the photo or collection pack you select.

Export, deletion, and retention

Collection packs include original photos. Clearing all collections requires two confirmations; the app has no server copy.

Changes

If cloud sync, analytics, ads, or AI services are added, this policy will be updated before the new version.
